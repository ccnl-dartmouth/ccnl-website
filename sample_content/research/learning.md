How do people and animals adjust what they learn when the world keeps changing? We build and test models in which
learning rates themselves adapt to how volatile and uncertain the environment seems.

Our experiments combine reward-learning tasks with computational modeling, so that the same model explains choices,
response times and, with collaborators, neural recordings.
