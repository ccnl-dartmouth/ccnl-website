---
headline: Computational & Cognitive Neuroscience Lab
---
Our research seeks to understand biological intelligence by uncovering the computational principles and neural mechanisms of learning and decision making. We focus on elucidating the cognitive processes underlying adaptive behavior, as well as the cognitive heuristics and biases that humans and other animals adopt to overcome challenges in naturalistic environments. Our research combines computational methods and human experiments, and ranges from synaptic and circuit level models to analysis of individual and social behavior. We regularly collaborate with experimentalists who study mice, rats, monkeys, and humans using a variety of techniques.
