Adaptive behavior depends on neural mechanisms across multiple levels (synaptic, cellular, and circuit), each contributing to flexibility and adaptation at distinct timescales. To understand these mechanisms, we develop computational models spanning from synaptic to system levels, designed to explain both behavioral and neural data while making testable predictions.

We also develop computational methods to quantify behavioral and neural adjustments, and design more naturalistic experimental paradigms.
