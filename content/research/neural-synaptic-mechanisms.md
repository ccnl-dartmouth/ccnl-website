Flexible learning must be implemented by biological brains, requiring compromises between fast learning and precise estimation. We investigate the neural mechanisms required for learning by developing synaptic and circuit models, including reward-dependent metaplasticity, recurrent neural networks, and large-scale functional spiking networks, and test them against recordings and causal manipulations obtained with collaborators. This includes comparison to single-neuron timescales in monkeys, rats and mice, as well as large-scale activity patterns in monkeys and humans.
