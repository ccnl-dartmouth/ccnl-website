Choices depend on context, attention and expectation, and prospect theory describes simple gambles well. How values are constructed for complex options, at which stage reward influences perception and decision, and why expectations persist remain unclear. We investigate gambling behavior in humans and monkeys across a wide variety of tasks, then use reinforcement-learning and Bayesian models to fit their choices and expectation ratings.
